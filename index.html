About Me

I’m Keagan, an electrical engineering student with an extensive background in Python and C/C++, which I’ve been writing since I was a kid. I’m working toward a career as an embedded systems engineer, where software meets real hardware and every byte and clock cycle counts. I’m also fascinated by wireless telecommunications and RF antenna design, and I’m always looking for ways to learn more about how devices talk to each other over the air.

Along the way I’ve been involved in some projects I’m proud of, including Pypush, Beeper, PyTextNow, OpenHaystack, and reverse engineering the Gabb Phone. I’ve self-hosted many different services, and I volunteer at BYU-Idaho’s Security Operations Center for fun. You can see a few of my projects on the right, and you can read more about my background on the About Me page.
